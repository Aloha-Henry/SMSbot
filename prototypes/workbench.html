    SURFACE 04

    Flags

    Operational flags ask you to make a phone call. Evidentiary flags ask you to make a judgment about the case — those are attorney-only, and the SLA clock on them is a legal artifact, not a UI nicety.

    Switch the role toggle to ATTORNEY to see the evidentiary flags unlock. An unacknowledged flag sitting inside a hash-chained record is proof the firm was on notice — which is why the clock is visible.
