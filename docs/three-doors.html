PIRRAPRIVILEGE ARCHITECTUREWORKING DIAGRAMAUG 2026

Defense counsel reaches the record through three doors. The architecture locks two and hands the attorney the key to the third.

Privilege and work product are what counsel asserts. The architecture is what makes the assertion true. Twilio retention, plane separation and the export path are where the assertion is actually won or lost.

Every architecture piece, the door it locks, and why

Written direction letter + protocol object, timestampedDOOR 1Establishes counsel's direction. This is the Heppner ground the design answers head-on.
The communications actually reach counsel, and the client is told soDOOR 1The fight is not how often the lawyer replies. It is whether she was communicating with her lawyer or with a vendor. Zero-retention, no-training model terms; tenant isolationDOOR 1 DOOR 2Confidentiality expectation. Heppner lost partly on consumer terms of service.
Twilio Message Redaction on (unredacted data accessible to Twilio production ≤24 h; compliance copy retained with limited access; media ≤7 days); BAA; enterprise termsDOOR 2A third-party copy is a subpoena target and weakens the confidentiality claim for everything.
Vendor subpoena protocol in the firm contractDOOR 2PIRRA notifies the firm and produces nothing without a court order; the firm asserts privilege on the client's behalf.
Liability steering: never asks, never pursues, routes to counselDOOR 3Limits what is in the record before anyone elects to open it. Still committed, never deleted.
The §771 prep protocol: attorney works from the record, client never studies itDOOR 3California makes production mandatory for writings a witness used to refresh, including before testifying. This is the most likely accidental opening.
Hash chain, append-only, independent timestampsNONEInsurance, not a lock. If privilege fails or when counsel elects, the record is complete, authenticated and credible. And nothing can be deleted, so spoliation is off the table.

Boiled all the way down: privilege and work product are what counsel asserts; the architecture is what makes the assertion true; the export path and Twilio's retention settings are where it is actually won or lost; and the only door that should ever open is the one the attorney chooses to open.
