From the first text to the exhibit: one case, start to finish.

Three lanes. The client only ever sees a text thread. PIRRA captures, structures, and spots issues. The firm directs, supervises, and exports. Everything in the left two lanes lands in one tamper-evident record; everything the firm does on top of it is the firm's work.

YESCLIENT_STATEMENT · LOG-0001
CONSENT RECORDED · 2026-09-04 09:12:41 HST · text hash 9f3a…c21e · notice state → DISCLOSED_CONSENTED
Thank you. Before we get into today, help me understand what a normal day looked like for you before the accident.AGENT_PROMPT · ONBOARDING BEGINS
  • Every disclosure the rules ask for is in message two, in the order a person would want to hear it: what I am, who I work for, where your words go, that they can be used, what to do in an emergency, how to stop. Nothing is buried in a link.
  • "Leilani decides whether and how" is deliberate (revised Sept 5). It states the one purpose: collected confidentially for counsel, released only on counsel's decision. It replaces v1.1's blunter "may be used in your case," which an outside reviewer read as in tension with confidentiality at the moment of communication; whether the sharpened notice is still needed is a counsel question. A client who understands the thread is part of their file gives more accurate answers, and the defense loses the "she thought it was private" attack.
  • The attorney is named, not just the firm. That is what makes "directed by your attorney" a fact the client can check, and it is what the Rule 1.4 disclosure comment expects.
  • Nothing captures before YES. A STOP ends it and is logged. A question ("is this really from Leilani?") goes to the paralegal's inbox as a human task, and PIRRA does not answer it.
  • No warmth theater. Message one is friendly because it is plain, not because it pretends. The landing page's line applies here: warmth from craft, not from pretending to be human.
  • Consent is a system event, not a client statement. It carries the exact disclosure text version, so the firm can always show what the client agreed to.

STATE

Two state machines run under the whole flow

Notice and consent, per client

NOT_STARTED→DISCLOSED_PENDING→DISCLOSED_CONSENTED→PAUSED/STOPPED→CLOSED

Capture is only possible in DISCLOSED_CONSENTED. Every transition is a system event with the disclosure version attached.

Certification readiness, per export (v1.1 §4.7)

CERTIFICATION_ELIGIBLE→NOTICE_NOT_SERVED→NOTICE_SERVED→OBJECTION_RECEIVED→LIVE_FOUNDATION_REQUIRED

The packaging engine refuses to describe a certification route as ready until the pinned jurisdiction pack's notice requirements are satisfied. The live-foundation kit is always the fallback.

WHAT THE FLOW PROVES

Where the supervision comes from

Nobody in this flow performs a task called "supervision." The attorney sets up the case (Stage 1), reads a digest (Stage 7), taps on flags (Stage 7), and elects at export (Stage 8). The record of Rule 5.3 supervision is produced underneath those four ordinary acts:
