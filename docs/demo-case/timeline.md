**What the dual dating shows.** 46 entries. 30 carry a present-state statement at the moment of
reporting and sit under §1250 on their face. Nine are past-fact statements with no independent
California vehicle when offered by the plaintiff (§1251 requires unavailability; §1241 does not
reach a statement made after the conduct, and this record does not establish the interval for any
of them). Those entries are not useless: they identify corroborating records, they refresh
recollection under §771 with its production consequence, the client can testify to them from
personal knowledge (§702), §1237 may allow a statement to be read if its foundation is met (the
writing itself comes in only if the adverse party offers it), and §791(a)/§1236 opens a route if
she is impeached. Two are double hearsay (T-12, T-17) and point at provider records. The remainder
are record facts or third-party-contact facts.

**Trustworthiness.** §1252 excludes a §1250 statement "made under circumstances such as to indicate
its lack of trustworthiness." The defense argument writes itself: statements to a tool provided by
the plaintiff's lawyer, after retention, with notice that counsel decides whether and how they are used. The answers
in this record are the fixed library (52 of 52 agent turns cite a prompt id, none composed), the
balanced prompts, the three adverse entries kept, the two unlocated scores left unlocated, and the
two sourced-slot follow-ups that quote her own words back rather than suggest new ones. Whether that
is enough is a counsel question, not a design claim.

**The conversation rule.** Revision 1 said the library should "always follow an event with a
present-state question." Withdrawn. The rule in v1.4 is to capture the account first and let a
present-state question follow where it is natural. In this revision the client volunteered a
present score after both open invitations (T-23, T-31) without being asked.

**The log itself.** §1271 on custodian foundation gets the record in; §1201 means each embedded
client statement still needs its own row above. When the *defendant* offers any client statement,
§1220 and none of this is needed.
