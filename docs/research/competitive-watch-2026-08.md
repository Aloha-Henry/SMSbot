# Competitive watch — Quilia re-check (late Aug 2026)

Update to `docs/source/PIRRA-Competitive-Scan-Aug2026.md` (preserved as written). Sources:
Quilia public changelog, product pages, and app-store listings via web search; quilia.com is
blocked by this environment's proxy so the changelog itself was not read directly — spot-check
before repeating any specific claim externally.

## What Quilia shipped or announced (2026 releases)

- **AI document intelligence** — analyzes client-uploaded files, auto-organizes.
- **AI case summaries + a morning AI digest** of client activity.
- **Treatment-gap detection** — "AI helps by identifying signals that treatment has stalled or
  deviated from expectations," surfaced while the case is active. **"Case Signal" (announced,
  not shipped): "insights that help your team fill in gaps before they appear."**
- Pain-score logging in the app — "accurate, time-stamped data."
- Photo uploads from the client's phone — including, per their own marketing, **"photos of
  witnesses or other drivers."**
- SmartAdvocate integration; "every CMS" positioning; inactivity monitoring and engagement
  badges on the attorney dashboard.
- **A family-law case type** — horizontal drift beyond PI.
- SMS used as deep links **into the app** — SMS is their notification channel, not their capture
  channel.

## Tripwire status (per the Aug scan: "exhibit," "FRE," "902," "1006," "provenance," "privilege")

**NOT crossed.** No exhibit, certification, authentication, provenance, privilege, or
work-product language anywhere found. Their evidence story remains "documentation that
strengthens demand letters." No conversational AI check-in shipped — their AI is analysis-side
(summaries, document triage, gap signals), and the client is still self-logging in an app.

## The two honest erosions

1. **"Catch the treatment gap" is now a contested feature, not a differentiator.** Quilia
   detects stalled treatment; EvenUp's agents flag missed appointments. Landing section G's
   pitch survives (ours is tied to an acknowledgment workflow that produces a documented
   paper-trail), but it can no longer carry differentiation weight alone — same trajectory as
   "we're conversational" in the Aug scan.
2. **They are converging on our inputs.** Time-stamped pain scores, photos, timelines "ready
   for demand preparation." The input side of the sliver is crowding.

## What has NOT moved — the durable differences

1. **App vs. no-app.** Quilia requires download + login + self-directed logging — the failed
   journal behavior with better UX. Their own dashboards for inactivity monitoring are the
   tell: they have an engagement problem to monitor. Density is our evidentiary quality metric,
   and SMS-native capture is how density happens.
2. **Self-logged data vs. an evidence architecture.** "Time-stamped" is not tamper-evident,
   and a pain-score CSV is not an exhibit with an authentication plan. Nothing on their side:
   custodian certification, hash chain + independent timestamps, notice mechanics, two-layer
   hearsay analysis, candidate pathways, adverse-entry disclosure, rehabilitation finder.
3. **No attorney-direction layer.** No counsel authorization artifact, no conversation modes,
   no decline election, no liability steering. Nothing that makes the capture *directed*.
4. **No containment — the opposite.** They invite uploads of "photos of witnesses or other
   drivers": scene/liability content flowing into a discoverable app with no discipline. That
   is the exact material our steering + exhibit-surface exclusion exists to manage.
5. **Their marketing frames demand-building as the purpose** ("capturing and logging treatment
   and documentation automatically" to "build stronger demand letters"). Our language rules ban
   that framing because it is a *Palmer* gift — vendor marketing is a defense exhibit about why
   the records exist. They are writing the defense's brief against their own users' data.
6. **Horizontal vs. narrow.** Family law says general client-portal play. We stay in the sliver.

## The line for the pitch

Quilia hands your client a form and hopes they fill it in; PIRRA holds the conversation and
builds the record. Quilia's data ends up "ready for demand preparation" — their words; ask them
who authenticates it, and how. (Do not name them on the live page; category language only.)
